What is
THE FORGE?

THE FORGE is a monthly founder talk series at KTH. One operator teaches a room of 100 what they wish they'd known before building. The talk is the product. Everything else serves it.

01

Not a cohort. No applications, no tiers, no gatekeeping.

02

Not a curriculum. Topics bend to the speaker.

03

Not an accelerator. No capital, no equity, no office space.

04

Not a podcast. Video stays private. The page is public.

05

Free. Food included. No tickets. No sponsors on the stage.

06

Ambition is the only filter. The room belongs to you.

Time Session What happens
01 12:30 Settle Lunch is at the back. Find a seat. The room fills fast - 100 seats, one session a month.
02 12:30 – 13:30 Fireside One founder on stage. One topic. Unscripted. What they wish they'd known before building.
03 13:30 – 14:00 Q&A The room asks. The founder answers. No moderation. No soft questions. Operator-direct.
04 14:00 Done. No drinks reception. No networking hour. The talk is the product. Go build something.
